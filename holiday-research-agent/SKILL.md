# Holiday Research Agent

## Overview

Structured methodology for comparing package holidays across multiple operators. Core principle: **never trust brochure descriptions alone**—always cross-verify against guest reviews, operator FAQs, and community feedback. Confidence scoring explicitly separates verified facts from reasonable inferences.

## When to Use

- Comparing holidays across multiple operators (TUI, Jet2, EasyJet Holidays, First Choice, Love Holidays, On The Beach, Kuoni)
- Validating all-inclusive package claims (especially drinks inclusion, quality, variety)
- Family-oriented resort research where specific requirements matter (waterpark, kids club, entertainment)
- Budget-constrained decisions where trade-offs require careful evaluation
- Cross-checking conflicting information from brochures vs. reviews

## When NOT to Use

- Last-minute bookings with <24 hours to research (insufficient time for verification)
- Resorts where reviews are insufficient (<50 recent reviews) and operator won't confirm details
- Tasks where you must provide live availability or instant booking (use operator websites directly)

## Time Budget Guidance

**Expected research duration:**
- **Per hotel:** 10-15 minutes (4-5 min brochure + FAQ, 8-10 min reviews)
- **3-4 hotel comparison:** 60-90 minutes total
- **Shortlist + rank:** 15-20 minutes additional

**Factors that increase time:**
- Hotel has 300+ reviews (will take longer to sample + verify patterns)
- Conflicting review information (need to investigate outliers)
- Low confidence required = more reviews needed to verify
- Multiple hidden requirements (dietary, accessibility, specific kids' activities)

**Time-saving hacks:**
- If you find conflicting info quickly, mark LOW confidence and move on (don't spend 20 min resolving one unclear point)
- Use operator-specific FAQ searches (Ctrl+F "cocktails", "spirits") rather than reading full FAQ
- Once you've found 3-4 HIGH/MEDIUM confidence options that meet requirements, stop searching — diminishing returns

**Quality vs. speed trade-off:**
- **60 min budget:** Compare 3 hotels, shortlist top 2, acceptable confidence level
- **90 min budget:** Compare 4-5 hotels, verify one HIGH confidence option, medium confidence runners-up
- **<30 min:** Only compare if hotels are very similar / clear standout exists

## Quick Start (TL;DR)

If you're comparing 3-4 hotels in 60 minutes:

1. **Brochure phase (5 min):** Collect price, star rating, facilities, flight/transfer times
2. **Review search (40 min):** For EACH hotel, search reviews for: "cocktails/premium spirits" (all-inclusive verification), "kids club" (age range), "waterpark" (slides, kids pool), "transfer time"
3. **Validation (10 min):** Reject immediately if: cocktails mentioned as extra cost in ANY review, or kids club age doesn't match your requirements
4. **Ranking (5 min):** Sort by: HIGH confidence first, then family-fit match, then price

**Skip the full framework for small decisions.** Use it when: multiple hotels with conflicting info, expensive decision, or high-stakes family requirements (specific age ranges, dietary needs).

## Research Framework: The Verification Cycle

All research follows this cycle for EACH hotel:

```
BROCHURE → OPERATOR FAQ → REVIEWS → CROSS-CHECK → CONFIDENCE SCORE → RANK
```

### Step 1: Collect Brochure Claims

Source: Official operator pages (TUI.com, Jet2holidays.com, etc.)

Document EXACTLY:
- Hotel name, country, location
- Package price (date-specific)
- Star rating (operator's claim)
- All-inclusive inclusions (as stated)
- Facilities listed
- Transfer/flight times stated
- Kids facilities described

**Trap to avoid:** Don't assume brochure comprehensiveness. "Includes alcohol" without specifying types is meaningless.

### Step 2: Check Operator FAQ & T&Cs

Source: Operator website support pages, booking terms, all-inclusive policy documents

Look for:
- What "all-inclusive" specifically means (does it include premium spirits? Cocktails?)
- Drink package details (brands, upgrade options, limitations)
- Transfer time accuracy (stated vs. actual)
- Hidden costs (deposits, upgrades, activity charges)
- Cancellation/change policies

**Trap to avoid:** Operators use deceptive language:
- "Premium brands available" = you can BUY them at full price
- "Premium brands at select bars" = upgraded areas only, not your room/beach bar
- "Premium options available for upgrade" = explicitly NOT included
- "Cocktails available" = you pay for them
- "Cocktails included in all-inclusive" = what you want to see

**Operator-specific nuances:**
- **TUI:** Generally clear on all-inclusive details, but T&Cs bury hidden costs (activity fees, resort transfers). Always check "What's Not Included"
- **Jet2:** More transparent on drinks packages than competitors. FAQ usually specifies exact spirit brands included
- **First Choice:** Known for vague all-inclusive definitions in brochures. Heavy reliance on FAQ + reviews to clarify. Often "premium spirits at bar but not in room"
- **EasyJet Holidays:** Competitive pricing but all-inclusive can be thin (basic drinks only). Reviews essential
- **Love Holidays / On The Beach:** Often offer same hotels as competitors at different prices. Focus on price comparison across operators once hotel shortlist is confirmed
- **Cancellation/change policies vary wildly:** Jet2 typically more flexible. TUI stricter but cheaper. Check before shortlisting if flexibility matters

### All-Inclusive Tier Reference

⚠️ **Critical:** Operators do NOT standardize all-inclusive tier names. Same resort may be "All-Inclusive" at TUI but "All-Inclusive Plus" at Jet2. You MUST verify contents via operator FAQ, not trust tier names.

| Tier Name | Typical Contents | Spirits? | Cocktails? | Premium Brands? | Who Uses It | Red Flag |
|-----------|-----------------|----------|-----------|-----------------|------------|----------|
| **All-Inclusive** | Beer, wine, soft drinks, basic spirits | Maybe | No | No | All operators | Weakest tier—assume spirits not included unless reviews confirm |
| **All-Inclusive Plus** | Beer, wine, spirits, maybe cocktails | Yes | Maybe | Sometimes | TUI, Jet2 | Check FAQ—"Plus" varies wildly. Jet2 Plus ≠ TUI Plus |
| **Ultra All-Inclusive** | Beer, wine, spirits, cocktails, premium brands | Yes | Yes | Yes | First Choice, some TUI | Premium option—verify premium brands in FAQ. Still may exclude top-shelf |
| **Premium/Deluxe All-Inclusive** | Same as Ultra usually, sometimes premium snacks | Yes | Yes | Yes | Luxury operators (Kuoni, Sunvil) | Most comprehensive. Confirm premium brand list |

**How to use this table:**
1. When you see "All-Inclusive Plus" in brochure, DON'T assume it includes cocktails
2. Instead: Find operator FAQ, search for "cocktails", "premium spirits", specific brand names
3. Cross-check with 3+ reviews confirming what's actually included
4. If FAQ is vague (e.g., "premium options available"), mark LOW confidence on drinks quality

**Example red flag:**
```
Brochure: "Ultra All-Inclusive with premium spirits"
FAQ: Silent on which spirits
Reviews: "Cocktails cost extra", "Only basic spirits included"

→ Tier name is MEANINGLESS. Trust reviews over tier name.
→ Mark this hotel as "All-Inclusive Basic" not "Ultra"
```

### Step 3: Validate Against Reviews - The Core Step

Source: TripAdvisor, Google Reviews, operator-specific review sites, recent guest blogs

**Review sampling strategy:** For hotels with 200+ reviews, don't read all. Instead:
- Filter by **2024-2025 reviews only** (ignore older)
- Read **newest 20 reviews** first (most current)
- If you find conflicting info, scan 10 more to spot patterns
- If reviews are consistent across your sample, stop
- Never extrapolate from <3 reviews on a specific topic

**Review quality signals — prioritize high-confidence sources:**
- ✅ **Highest confidence:** Reviews with **photos** of the pools/kids club/food (shows actual current state)
- ✅ **High confidence:** Reviews from **families with children** (parent perspective, not couple/adult travelers)
- ✅ **High confidence:** **400+ total reviews + 4.0+ rating** (large sample, less likely to be fluky)
- ⚠️ **Medium confidence:** **50-400 reviews, 3.5+ rating** (smaller sample, may have outliers)
- ❌ **Low confidence:** **<50 reviews OR <3.5 rating** (too small or consistently negative — re-evaluate entire hotel)
- ❌ **Skip:** Single reviews with unusual claims (e.g., one person says "no kids club" when others confirm it exists — likely user error)
- ❌ **Deprioritize:** Non-UK travelers (expectations for all-inclusive, family atmosphere differ significantly — see bias note below)

**Non-UK traveler bias warning:** German, Dutch, and Scandinavian families have different all-inclusive expectations than UK families:
- Germans expect beer/wine only in basic all-inclusive (spirits are paid)
- UK families expect spirits + cocktails included
- **When reading non-UK reviews:** Adjust expectations. "Good all-inclusive" from a German reviewer may mean "spirits not included". Always look for UK-specific reviews or ask "would a UK family consider this acceptable?"
- **Weighting:** If you have 5 UK reviews and 15 German reviews saying drinks are basic, trust the UK reviews for UK family suitability
- **Red flag:** If ONLY non-UK travelers reviewed the resort, mark LOW confidence for family suitability (insufficient UK perspective)

**For EACH requirement, search reviews for:**

| Requirement | Review Search Terms | Red Flags |
|-------------|-------------------|-----------|
| **Drinks inclusion** | "cocktails", "spirits", "premium", "drinks", "bar", "charge for" | Mentions of "upgrade", "extra cost for", "weak drinks", "limited selection" |
| **Waterpark quality** | "pool", "slides", "kids pool", "splash", "lazy river" | "Crowded", "broken", "limited", "disappointing" |
| **Kids club suitability** | "kids club", "children's activities", "entertainment", "babysitting" | Age mismatches, "not suitable for 7-8", "limited activities", "poor supervision" |
| **Food quality** | "food", "meals", "buffet", "variety", "pizza", "ice cream" | "Repetitive", "poor quality", "limited choices", "always the same" |
| **Transfer experience** | "transfer", "coach", "time", "airport", "journey" | "2 hours", "3 hours", "delayed", "overcrowded" |
| **Family atmosphere** | "British families", "UK guests", "English-speaking", "family-friendly", "kids" | "Adult-focused", "quiet", "no activities for children" |

**Confidence indicators in reviews:**
- ✅ **High confidence:** Multiple recent reviews (2024-2025) all mention the same detail consistently
- ⚠️ **Medium confidence:** Some reviews mention it, others silent (possible variation or older info)
- ❌ **Low confidence:** Conflicting reviews, older reviews (2022+), single mention

**Example red flag sequence:**
```
Brochure says: "All-inclusive with premium spirits"
Operator FAQ silent on which spirits
Review 1 (Jan 2025): "Cocktails charged extra"
Review 2 (Dec 2024): "Only beer, wine, local spirits included"
Review 3 (Nov 2024): "Premium drinks cost extra"

→ CONFIDENCE: LOW - Assume cocktails are NOT included
```

### Step 4: Cross-Check Conflicting Information

When brochure ≠ reviews, follow this priority:

1. **Recent guest reviews (2024-2025)** - These are actual current experience
2. **Multiple consistent mentions** - If 3+ independent reviews say it, trust them
3. **Operator FAQ details** - More reliable than marketing copy
4. **Single review mentions** - Could be outdated, user error, or unusual
5. **Brochure claims alone** - Lowest priority (marketing language)

**Example resolution:**
```
Brochure: "All-inclusive with alcohol"
5 recent reviews: "Cocktails cost €8-12 extra"
Operator FAQ: Silent

→ CONCLUSION: Cocktails NOT included in all-inclusive
→ CONFIDENCE: High (multiple recent sources)
→ ACTION: Reject hotel (requirement not met)
```

### Step 5: Build Confidence Score

For EACH hotel, assign confidence level:

**HIGH Confidence:**
- ✅ Core requirements clearly verified across multiple 2024-2025 reviews
- ✅ No conflicting information
- ✅ Operator FAQ aligns with reviews
- ✅ Specific details confirmed (e.g., "kids club age 6-12", "cocktails included")

**MEDIUM Confidence:**
- ⚠️ Most requirements confirmed, 1-2 with reasonable inferences
- ⚠️ Some reviews mention detail, others silent (assume consistency)
- ⚠️ Operator FAQ partially supports claims
- ⚠️ Example: "No reviews mention weak drinks package" ≠ "drinks package is premium"

**LOW Confidence:**
- ❌ Key requirements not verifiable from reviews
- ❌ Conflicting review information
- ❌ Heavy reliance on brochure claims with no review support
- ❌ Insufficient recent reviews (all 2023 or older)
- ❌ Operator deliberately vague in FAQ ("premium brands available")

## Validation Rules (Apply These Strictly)

### All-Inclusive Drinks Package

**REJECT immediately if (MANDATORY):**
- Cocktails mentioned as chargeable extra in ANY recent review (even one review saying "extra cost" = NOT included)
- Reviews repeatedly mention "weak" or "basic" drink selection
- Operator FAQ doesn't explicitly list what's included
- Only basic beer, wine, and house spirits with no premium brands mentioned
- Brochure says "premium options available" or "premium brands at bar" (this means NOT in all-inclusive)

**For HIGH Confidence, ALL of these must be true:**
- Minimum 3 recent guest reviews (2024-2025) specifically praising drinks quality OR naming included brands
- ZERO reviews mentioning "extra charge" or "upgrade" for cocktails/premium spirits
- Operator FAQ explicitly lists included brands by name (e.g., "Bacardi, Smirnoff, José Cuervo")
- Reviews confirm cocktails are included (not just "available at bar")

**For MEDIUM Confidence, 2 of these must be true:**
- 2 recent reviews mention included drinks, but brands not specific
- Operator FAQ confirms "cocktails included" without naming brands
- Reviews mention good drinks selection but no explicit "premium spirits" confirmation
- One strong positive review + brochure claim, but limited review coverage

**ALWAYS mark LOW Confidence if:**
- Brochure says "premium spirits included" but reviews don't confirm which spirits
- Only 1 review mentions drinks, others silent
- Operator FAQ silent or vague ("premium options available")
- <3 recent reviews total about drinks quality
- Conflicting information between reviews

### Family Suitability

**Mandatory for waterpark:**
- Main pool (for adults)
- Kids pool (separate)
- Minimum 2 slides OR splash features
- Lazy river is bonus, not required

**Mandatory for kids entertainment:**
- Kids club suitable for ages 7-8 (confirm from reviews, not just brochure)
- Evening children's entertainment (kids shows, activities)
- Daytime family activities (not adults-only clubs)

**Mandatory for family atmosphere:**
- Evidence from reviews that UK families attend (British names, "family atmosphere")
- English-speaking staff mentioned positively
- No reviews complaining about "too many British tourists" (that's actually what we want)

### Budget Validation

**If hotel is within budget range (stated max, e.g., £3,500):**
- ✅ Include in ranking
- Document any uncertainty (e.g., "baggage may be extra £X per person")

**If hotel exceeds budget — strict criteria to justify overspend:**

Over-budget options included ONLY if they satisfy at least ONE of these:

1. **Adds a mandatory requirement your budget hotels don't have**
   - Example: Budget hotels have NO waterpark; over-budget option HAS waterpark (your kids need this)
   - Include, mark "OVER BUDGET: +£400 (only option with waterpark)"
   - Example counterexample: Budget hotels have waterpark, over-budget has BETTER waterpark but same functionality
   - Reject (no mandatory gap)

2. **Dramatically improves all-inclusive quality** (worth £200+ family savings)
   - Example: Budget hotels: beer/wine only (cocktails £10+ extra). Over-budget: cocktails included
   - Calculate: 10 cocktails × £12/each = £120 savings. Add to cost comparison
   - Include if net cost (price + extras) is still reasonable

3. **Reduces transfer time by 45+ minutes** (meaningful vacation time recovery)
   - Example: Budget hotel 2.5-hour transfer, over-budget 1-hour transfer
   - Value: +1.5 hours family time on arrival/departure days = realistic family benefit
   - Include if worth the time trade

**For all over-budget inclusions:**
- Mark clearly: "OVER BUDGET: +£500 justified by: [MANDATORY REQUIREMENT / QUALITY / TIME]"
- Explain exact value added (not "better experience", but "cocktails included = £150 savings" or "45 min shorter transfer")
- Flag if you're stretching budget without meeting above criteria (acknowledge this is a compromise)

**Red flag:** "It just looks nicer overall" is NOT a justification. The must-have requirements are, budget is a constraint.

## Ranking Criteria (NOT by price alone)

**Primary factors (weight: 60%):**
1. Confidence score (High > Medium > Low)
2. All-inclusive drinks verification (essential)
3. Family requirements match (waterpark, kids club, entertainment)

**Secondary factors (weight: 25%):**
1. UK/English suitability evidence
2. Value within budget (price relative to features)
3. Transfer/flight times

**Tertiary factors (weight: 15%):**
1. Bonus features (lazy river, swim-up room, refurbished)
2. Review enthusiasm (not just compliance)

**Formula:** Rank by (Confidence × Family-Fit × Verified-All-Inclusive) ÷ (Price-Index)

## Seasonal & Timing Considerations

**Review recency varies by season:**
- **June-August reviews** are most relevant for summer bookings (peak family season)
- **May reviews** may be older (April travelers) — deprioritize slightly if newer reviews exist
- **Winter reviews** (Nov-Feb) often reflect different guest mix (older couples, fewer families) — weight family-specific reviews higher
- **School holiday pricing:** Easter/summer/Christmas reviews often reflect surge pricing (families pay more). Budget estimates may look worse than they actually are

**Hotel changes over time:**
- If reviews jump from mixed 2023 → highly positive 2024+, hotel likely refurbished. Trust newer reviews
- If all recent reviews are from same season (only July-Aug 2024), you're missing winter/shoulder-season feedback. Mark as incomplete data if booking for different season
- Ownership/management changes can shift quality dramatically. If reviews are inconsistent, ask when management changed

**Currency fluctuations:** If booking destination with volatile currency (Turkish Lira, Egyptian Pound), all-inclusive deals look better in stronger-pound years. Check when brochure prices were last updated

**Booking window:** Most operators release prices 9-12 months ahead. Comparing July 2026 holidays now? You're at peak research window. Comparing next month? Expect fewer reviews (last-minute bookers); use more recent non-review sources

## Booking & Price Comparison Strategy

Once you've shortlisted 2-3 hotels (HIGH/MEDIUM confidence, family requirements met):

1. **Check same hotel across operators:**
   - Same resort often listed by TUI, Jet2, Love Holidays at different prices
   - Use comparison sites (Holiday Hypermarket, Holiday Extras) to spot which operator is cheapest for YOUR dates
   - Factor in: baggage allowance (Jet2 includes 2 bags, TUI charges), transfer included vs. extra, cancellation flexibility

2. **Hidden cost audit:**
   - Baggage: Jet2 free (2 bags), TUI/First Choice usually paid (£25-40 per person)
   - Airport parking: Factor in if driving (£50-150 for week)
   - Upgrades: Resort levies charges for partial premium rooms, swim-up, beachfront
   - Activity bookings: Kids clubs often chargeable despite "free entertainment" claim

3. **Exchange rate impact:** If destination uses non-GBP currency, check operator's lock rate. Some fix price at booking, others float until 14 days before travel. Matters for volatile currencies

4. **All-inclusive quality comparison:** Don't assume all "all-inclusive" packages of same price are equal
   - Resort A: Spirits included, cocktails charged (£10/each)
   - Resort B: Beer/wine/soft drinks included, spirits not included
   - Same price point, different value for your family

## Tie-Breaker Rules

When two hotels are equally strong on confidence, family fit, and price — use these to decide:

**First tie-breaker:** **Non-negotiable requirements**
- Does this hotel have verified kids club for ages 7-8? → Pick the one that does (if other doesn't)
- Is waterpark actually good (3+ slides vs. 2), or just adequate? → Pick better waterpark

**Second tie-breaker:** **All-inclusive quality difference**
- If one includes cocktails + premium spirits and other doesn't, strongly favor the better package (worth £200+ in value to family)
- If both are same (e.g., both beer/wine only), move to next tie-breaker

**Third tie-breaker:** **Transfer/flight time**
- Shorter transfers = less vacation time wasted, kids sleep better → Pick shorter if <30 min difference
- If equal, move to next

**Fourth tie-breaker:** **Review enthusiasm (not just scores)**
- Read 3 reviews from each hotel shortlist
- Hotel A: "Fine, kids enjoyed it, drinks okay"
- Hotel B: "Brilliant! Food amazing, kids club brilliant, will book again"
- Enthusiasm isn't everything, but it suggests better experience overall → Slight preference to Hotel B

**Fifth tie-breaker:** **Staff/service mentions**
- UK reviews mentioning "friendly staff", "helpful", "English-speaking" suggest better family experience
- If one hotel has 3+ mentions of helpful English-speaking staff and other has none, slight preference to former

**Last resort tie-breaker:** **Price/value**
- Only use price to break final ties, not earlier (don't choose worse hotel to save £50)
- If truly tied on everything else, cheaper wins

## Common Mistakes & How to Fix Them

| Mistake | Fix |
|---------|-----|
| **Trust brochure "all-inclusive" without verification** | ALWAYS search reviews for "cocktails", "premium", "charge for" - if reviews say extra, it's extra |
| **Dismiss hotel due to single negative review** | Check if it's an outlier. 1 complaint in 100 reviews = not a pattern. 10 complaints in 100 = pattern |
| **Assume old reviews still apply** | 2023 reviews may be outdated. Prioritize 2024-2025. If <10 recent reviews, mark LOW confidence |
| **Confuse "available" with "included"** | Brochure: "premium spirits available" ≠ "premium spirits included". Review search is mandatory |
| **Skip operator FAQ** | FAQ often reveals exclusions. "Cocktails available at bar" = not included, not all-inclusive |
| **Over-weight UK suitability** | Don't pick a worse hotel just for UK guests. It's a tiebreaker, not primary criterion |
| **Accept "pool and kids club" without specifics** | Search reviews: what age is kids club? Is entertainment actually good? Don't assume |
| **Ignore transfer time inflation** | Brochure says "1.5 hours". Reviews say "2+ hours regularly". Trust reviews |
| **Mark MEDIUM confidence on uncertain items** | If you can't verify it and brochure is vague, it's LOW confidence, not medium |

## Red Flags - Stop and Investigate

These patterns in your research mean DO NOT proceed without clarification:

- ❌ Hotel brochure silent on kids club age range → Reviews don't mention it → CANNOT verify age 7-8 suitability → REJECT
- ❌ Multiple reviews mention "upgrade" for cocktails → Brochure says "all-inclusive" → CONFLICT → Assume NOT included
- ❌ No reviews from 2024-2025 → Only 2023 reviews exist → Hotel may have changed ownership/quality → LOW confidence
- ❌ Reviews praise drinks but specify only "basic spirits" → Claims contradict brochure "premium" claim → REJECT premium claim
- ❌ Temptation to mark MEDIUM confidence on unverified claims → "I couldn't find evidence BUT brochure says X" → That's LOW confidence, not MEDIUM. MEDIUM requires some review support.
- ❌ Hotel price £4,200 but you said £3,500 max → Temptation to "stretch budget" → DO NOT unless you explicitly accept overspend

## Rationalization Blockers (Common Shortcuts You'll Be Tempted to Take)

| Rationalization | Why It Fails | Correct Approach |
|-----------------|-------------|-----------------|
| **"Reviews don't mention cocktails, so I'll assume they're included"** | Silence ≠ confirmation. Review may not cover bar topic. | Mark LOW confidence unless 3+ reviews specifically confirm |
| **"One positive review said drinks were great, so HIGH confidence"** | One opinion ≠ pattern. Could be lucky timing. | Need minimum 3 corroborating reviews from recent travelers |
| **"Brochure says all-inclusive, so I'll assume spirits are included"** | Brochure marketing ≠ operational reality. Operators use vague language. | Require operator FAQ or review confirmation before trusting |
| **"Operator FAQ doesn't mention spirits, so maybe they're being conservative"** | No—silence in FAQ means they're NOT explicitly included. Assume NOT included. | If FAQ silent, assign LOW confidence for premium claims |
| **"I can't find reviews of that specific hotel, but it's a chain hotel and others are good"** | Chain brands vary by location and property. | Can't apply another resort's reputation. Find reviews of THIS specific resort. |
| **"The hotel looks good overall, I'll mark MEDIUM confidence on drinks"** | Overall quality ≠ drinks quality. Separate evaluation required. | Each requirement scored independently. Bad drinks = reject or LOW confidence |
| **"I spent 45 minutes researching, I should stop and provide recommendations"** | Time pressure is real but insufficient verification is worse than late delivery. | Better to say "I found 2 HIGH confidence options after 90 min" than 5 unverified ones |

- Brochure: "All-inclusive with premium spirits"
- Without review check: Assume it's included, book it
- Reality: Cocktails £12/each, premium spirits unavailable in all-inclusive
- Cost: Family spends £300+ on drinks upgrades instead of £0

**Confidence scoring prevents false certainty:**
- Hotel with HIGH confidence: Book with confidence
- Hotel with MEDIUM confidence: Ask operator for clarification before committing
- Hotel with LOW confidence: Keep looking for better-verified option

**Family suitability prevents missed requirements:**
- Brochure says "kids club" → You assume ages 6-12
- Reality: Kids club ages 10+, no activities for age 7
- With review validation: You catch this before booking
